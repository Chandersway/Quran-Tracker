warsh_ayah_map.json is a reference-only extract of number_in_hafs from Quran JSON:
https://quran-json.risanb.com/text/warsh/quran.json
https://github.com/risan/quran-json
Retrieved 2026-10-07. Source SHA-256 is included in the JSON.

Changes: omitted Arabic text and retained the verse-reference arrays, grouped
by chapter. No changes to verse references. Build and validation script:
scripts/build_warsh_ayah_map.py.

This mapping bridges Warsh/Madani verse numbering to the app's existing
Hafs-indexed text, word information, notes and bookmarks. It does not change
the spelling or numbering printed in the Warsh page images.
